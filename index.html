01 — THE IDEA

More than a website.
A complete experience.

JackOS turns the browser into a full operating-system-style environment. From the first boot to the desktop, every part is designed to feel intentional, responsive, and distinctly JackOS.

The project brings together a custom boot sequence, account setup, editions, app installation, security questions, login, desktop windows, and system UI.
